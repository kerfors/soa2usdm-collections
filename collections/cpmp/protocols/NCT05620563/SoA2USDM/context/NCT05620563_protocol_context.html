Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readSUB-STUDY OF A MASTER PROTOCOL. Page 1 reads verbatim: "This study H0P-MC-OA05 (NCT05620563) is a sub-study of Master Protocol H0P-MC-CPMP (NCT05986292)". The document is ISA H0P-MC-OA05, an intervention-specific appendix to Master Protocol H0P-MC-CPMP (p2, p16), used together with the OA disease-state appendix DSA CPMP(1) (H0P-MC-CPMP[1], p9). Nothing was filled from the master. Deferrals to the master protocol CPMP (and DSA CPMP(1)): SoA – 'Please refer to the Master Protocol CPMP and the OA DSA CPMP(1) SoAs for additional information' (p12, image) and footnote a 'Collect in addition to schedule described in Master Protocol CPMP and DSA CPMP(1)' bound to Concomitant medication, AE, C-SSRS Since Last Visit, Self-Harm Supplement form, Self-Harm Follow-Up form, NRS for pain, Rescue medication usage reporting, PGI and WOMAC® (p15, image); clinical laboratory tests – Appendix 2 lists only tests unique to ISA OA05 and states 'The Master Protocol CPMP describes tests that may be performed at additional times noted in the SoA for this ISA' (p34, image); section 8.2.3 'Additional details are provided in the Master Protocol CPMP' (p28). So the composition of Hematology, Chemistry, Lipid panel, Urinalysis and HbA1c (SoA rows) is not in this document and no panel was recorded for them; efficacy and safety assessments (p27), ECG additional details and QTcF withdrawal criteria (p27), AE/SAE/PC definitions (p28, p35), concomitant therapy (p24), discontinuation (p25-26), liver safety follow-up (p39), objectives and endpoints (p9, p18), eligibility (p21), lifestyle (p22), statistics (p31-32, image), pregnancy testing after screening (p37, image). VERSION (rule 11): the PDF holds a single version, Protocol H0P-MC-OA05 Amendment (a), approved 14-Oct-2022; the SoA (pp12-15) is from it. The Amendment Summary of Changes (p4-5) says amendment (a) added physical examinations at Visits 5 and 7 and the complete/targeted physical examination note in the SoA and Section 8.2.1, and removed '…and prior to PK sample collection' from the vital-signs note; these are already reflected in the extracted SoA and Section 8.2.1, so no additional entries were needed. PAGE IMAGES (rule 8): pages 12-15 (SoA), 31-32 (Section 9), 34 (Appendix 2 lab tests and 10.2.1), 36-38 (Appendix 4) have no text layer and were read from the page image; entries marked read_from 'page image'. The lab-tests table (p34) therefore rests on the image only (rule 10). Large parts of pp13-15 and p34 are redacted (CCI), including one SoA row under Scales/Questionnaires, one row under Clinical Laboratory Tests, the rows under Participant Devices and two abbreviations in the SoA abbreviation line. TEXT LAYER: pages 4, 7, 9, 16-22, 27, 30, 41, 43, 46 have a letter-spaced text layer (e.g. 'a d verse e ve nt'); quotes and abbreviation terms/definitions from those pages are copied as they stand in the text layer, so they carry the extra spaces. LAB: the hypersensitivity samples are 'assayed by Lilly-designated laboratory' (p34, image) while p29 says 'Laboratory results are provided to the sponsor via the central laboratory'; both recorded. Under exceptional circumstances 'Local laboratory testing may be conducted in lieu of central laboratory testing' (p41) - general, not attached to an SoA activity. Pregnancy testing: Appendix 4 states a negative serum test at screening followed by a negative urine result within 24 hours prior to treatment exposure (p37, image); the SoA row is urine pregnancy (V3 onward). UNDEFINED TOKENS in SoA activity names: 'C-SSRS' is not defined anywhere readable (a redacted abbreviation on p15 may be it); 'LY3857210' is the compound code; 'OA' and 'PGI', 'WOMAC', 'HbA1c' are defined only in the SoA abbreviation line (p15), not in Appendix 7. PROMIS is defined in Appendix 7 (p44) but no readable SoA row names it (likely the redacted row).
