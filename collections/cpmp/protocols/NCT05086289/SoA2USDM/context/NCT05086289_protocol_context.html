This document is the intervention-specific appendix (ISA) H0P-MC-BP02 to Master Protocol H0P-MC-CPMP (CPMP) and the CLBP disease-state appendix (DSA) H0P-MC-CPMP(2) (p2, p13); nothing was filled from the master. Deferrals to the master (as printed): SoA header (p11) 'Please refer to the CPMP Master Protocol and the CLBP DSA H0P-MC-CPMP(2) SoAs for additional information'; Appendix 1 (p29) 'The CPMP Master Protocol describes tests that may be performed at additional times noted in the SoA for this ISA. This table describes tests unique for ISA BP02.' - so the composition of SoA activities Hematology, Chemistry, Lipid panel, Urinalysis and HbA1c (and the specimen/lab of every lab activity) is not given in this document and is deferred to the master; objectives/endpoints (p8, p18), eligibility (p21), preparation/handling/storage (p22), concomitant therapy (p23), discontinuation (p24), AEs/SAEs (p25), statistics/populations (p27) are deferred to CPMP/CPMP(2). Section 8.2.3 (p25) refers to Section 10.1 (Appendix 1) for the clinical laboratory tests. Rule 10: the Appendix 1 lab-tests table (p29) was checked on the page image: one column 'Other Tests' with two rows (LY3526318 concentration; Urine pregnancy), followed by a CCI-redacted block; the image confirmed the text layer, no reassignment. p12 was viewed as an image: an SoA row between 'PK sample' and 'Participant Device' is CCI-redacted, and the first entry of the SoA abbreviation line is CCI-redacted - so 'dBM' (SoA note for 'Participant returns device') is not defined anywhere readable in the document. Many pages (e.g. 2, 7-9, 12-16, 19-23, 26-29, 34-36, 38) have a letter-spaced text layer; evidence quotes and the three SoA-table abbreviations from p12 (ED = early discontinuation; HbA1c = hemoglobin A1c; V = Visit) are kept exactly as the text layer spells them so the checker can verify them; target_heading and what_it_says give the readable wording. Instruments: none of this ISA's SoA activities is an instrument, so the list is empty; the Numeric Rating Scale (NRS) diary is mentioned (p19 'NRS diary entries', abbreviation p41) but has no SoA activity in this ISA (it belongs to the master/DSA SoAs). Panels: the only panel in this document is Appendix 1 'Other Tests'; no hematology/chemistry/lipid/urinalysis composition, specimen or central/local lab is stated. Labs: central vs local lab is not stated for any activity; Appendix 4 (p38) allows that 'Local laboratory testing may be conducted in lieu of central laboratory testing' only in exceptional circumstances. Pregnancy test conflict (rule 5): the SoA and Appendix 1 give a urine pregnancy test (p11, p29), while Appendix 2 (p31) states for WOCBP 'Negative urine result at screening followed by a negative serum result within 24 hours prior to treatment exposure. Intervention compliance (SoA activity) is described in Section 6.4 (p22: direct questioning, counting returned capsules) - not one of the six categories, so not recorded as an item. QTcF (p25) is not in the abbreviation list (only QTc).
