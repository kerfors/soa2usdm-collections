PROTOCOL VERSION: the PDF holds only Amendment (a) (approved 13-Oct-2022; original protocol 25-Aug-2022 is not included). The SoA notes come from this version (p12-15). The Summary of Changes (p4-5) records that the amendment added physical examinations at V5 and V7 and the complete/targeted PE note, removed '…and prior to PK sample collection' from the vital-signs note, and removed '…to physical examinations (described in Master Protocol CPMP)…'; the vital-signs change is cited as evidence in measurements. ABBREVIATIONS: Appendix 7 list (p44-45) plus abbreviations under the SoA (p15), schema (p11), intervention tables (p10, p23), liver stopping table (p25) and hypersensitivity lab table (p34).
