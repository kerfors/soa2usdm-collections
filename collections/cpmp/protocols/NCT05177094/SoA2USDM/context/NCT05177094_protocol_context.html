Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readSUB-STUDY OF A MASTER PROTOCOL. Page 1 reads verbatim: "This study H0P-MC-NP02 (NCT05177094) is a sub-study of Master Protocol H0P-MC-CPMP (NCT05986292)". This document is the intervention-specific appendix (ISA) H0P-MC-NP02 for LY3526318 to Master Protocol H0P-MC-CPMP; only this document was used, nothing was filled from the master protocol or the DPNP disease-state addendum (DSA) CPMP(3). What this document defers to the master protocol / DSA: the SoA itself (p11: the ISA SoA shows only visits and procedures unique to the ISA; 'Please refer to the CPMP Master Protocol and the DPNP DSA CPMP(3) SoAs for additional information'); clinical laboratory tests (p29, Appendix 1: 'The CPMP Master Protocol describes tests that may be performed at additional times noted in the SoA for this ISA' - the ISA table lists only 'Other Tests': LY3526318 concentration and Urine pregnancy). So the composition, specimen and lab of the SoA activities Hematology, Chemistry, Lipid panel, Urinalysis, Urine drug screen and HbA1c are not given in this document (deferred to the master protocol); no panels for them are recorded. Also deferred: objectives and endpoints (p8, p18), eligibility (p21), preparation/handling/storage (p22), concomitant therapy (p23), discontinuation (p24), adverse events (p25), statistical considerations (p27). Instruments: the SoA section 'Scales, Questionnaires, and Outcome Measures' (p11) is covered by a 'CCI' redaction box, so no SoA instrument rows are readable; the only instrument named in the text is the Numeric Rating Scale (NRS) diary (p19, Section 4.1). The text does not link the NRS to a named SoA activity, so its soa_activities is empty. The NRS abbreviation is defined differently in two places: 'Numeric Rating Scale' (Appendix 5 p41, and the p18 abbreviation line) vs 'numerical rating scale' (SoA abbreviation line p12); both are recorded. Note: Subsequent pregnancy testing is compound specific.'; the ISA SoA and Appendix 1 list only Urine pregnancy - both recorded in lab_and_purpose evidence, not resolved. SoA rows the text does not describe: Hematology, Chemistry, Lipid panel, Urinalysis, HbA1c (deferred to master), Scales, Questionnaires, and Outcome Measures (redacted), Participant returns device (redacted note).
