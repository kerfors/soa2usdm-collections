Page 1 reads verbatim: "This study H0P-MC-BP05 (NCT05630196) is a sub-study of Master Protocol H0P-MC-CPMP (NCT05986292)". The document is the intervention-specific appendix (ISA) H0P-MC-BP05 to Master Protocol H0P-MC-CPMP and the CLBP disease-state appendix CPMP(2) (pp. 2, 15). Nothing was filled from the master. Deferrals to the master printed in this document: the SoA shows only ISA-unique procedures and refers to the Master Protocol CPMP and CLBP DSA CPMP(2) SoAs (p. 11); footnote a (p. 14) - Concomitant medication, AE, C-SSRS Since Last Visit, Self-Harm Supplement form, Self-Harm Follow-Up form, NRS for pain, Rescue medication usage reporting and PGI are collected 'in addition to schedule described in the Master Protocol CPMP' - so instrument details (version, recall period, respondent, scoring) are not given here; efficacy and safety assessments (p. 26); ECG details and QTcF withdrawal criteria (p. 27); clinical laboratory tests - Appendix 2 lists only tests unique to BP05 and 'The Master Protocol CPMP describes tests that may be performed' (pp. 27, 33), so the composition of Hematology, Chemistry, Lipid panel, Urinalysis, HbA1c, Urine pregnancy and Urine drug screen is not stated in this document and panels holds only the BP05 tables; genetics (p. 29); AE/SAE definitions (pp. 27, 35); liver safety follow-up (p. 39); lifestyle, screen failures, discontinuation (pp. 21, 24). Protocol version: Protocol H0P-MC-BP05 Amendment (a), approved 13-Oct-2022; the PDF holds only this version (the Summary of Changes on p. 4 records that amendment a added physical examinations at V5 and V7 and the targeted-examination note; the SoA already includes them). Rule 10: the lab-tests table (p. 33) and the SoA abbreviation footer (p. 14) were checked against the page images; the text layer matched (Appendix 2 table has one row, LY3857210 concentration; a redacted CCI block follows it). No page lacks a text layer. Small differences recorded: PK = 'pharmacokinetic' under the SoA (p. 14) vs 'pharmacokinetics' in Appendix 10 (p. 43); Urine pregnancy: 'serum test result at screening followed by a negative urine result' (p. 37), SoA row is urine only. Tokens in SoA activity names not defined in the abbreviation list: C-SSRS (not expanded anywhere in the document); HbA1c and PGI are defined only in the SoA footer (p. 14); LY3857210 is the compound code. QTcF (p. 26) and eDiary (SoA note, p. 12) are not defined. SoA rows the text does not describe beyond the SoA itself: Confirm ISA inclusion and exclusion criteria, Prespecified CLBP medical history (SoA note only), Concomitant medication, AE, Placebo response video training (participant), Rescue medication usage reporting, Reinforce pain assessment guidelines (refers to MoO), Hematology, Chemistry, Lipid panel, Urinalysis, HbA1c, Urine drug screen (Section 5.3.1 only says when a positive screen is not illicit use). Intervention compliance is described on p. 22 (direct questioning and counting returned capsules) but is not an instrument or measurement. Urine pregnancy lab_and_purpose specimen rests on p. 37. The hypersensitivity panel's specimen 'blood' comes from Section 8.3.2 (p. 28), which also says results go to the sponsor via the central laboratory, while the table footnote names the Lilly-designated laboratory (p. 33). The physical examination components are those of the targeted examination; the content of the complete physical examination is not listed.
