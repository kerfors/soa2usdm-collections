Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readRule 11: the PDF holds one protocol version only, Protocol J2A-MC-GZGI(a) (Amendment a, approved 27-Sep-2021); the SoA (pp 13-18) is from this version. The amendment summary (pp 4-5) records changes relative to the original protocol (21-Jun-2021), e.g. vital signs changed from 3 to 2 sitting BP and PR measurements, ABPM and lipid measurements removed from exploratory efficacy assessments as safety assessments; no separate later amendment is in the PDF. Rule 10: the lab-tests table (Appendix 2, pp 84-87) was checked against the page images of pp 84, 85, 86 and 87; the text layer keeps the two columns (test, comment) in row order and the image confirmed every panel assignment, so nothing changed. Conflicts: (1) the SoA vital-signs note (p 14) lists pulse rate, blood pressure and temperature; Appendix 7 (p 100) lists BP and HR measured by pulse only, no temperature — both recorded. (2) PHQ-9 and C-SSRS sit under the SoA group header 'Clinician-Administered Assessments (Paper)' (p 15; the C-SSRS rows continue the group on p 16), while Section 8.3.3.10 (p 70) calls the PHQ-9 participant-completed. (3) The lipid objective (p 24) is still listed as exploratory while the amendment summary (p 5) says lipids measurements were removed from exploratory efficacy assessments as safety assessments. (4) Appendix 12 (p 111) defines CRP as clinical research physician; the SoA row 'High sensitivity CRP' uses CRP for something else that the protocol does not define. (5) The SoA abbreviation list (p 18) and Appendix 12 (p 111) define 'HBC = hepatitis B virus'; the SoA row uses HBV. (6) The lab-table abbreviation list (p 87) expands UACR as 'urine albumin/creatinine ration'; the SoA list (p 18) and Appendix 12 say 'urinary albumin creatinine ratio'. SoA rows the text does not describe: Glucagon, C-peptide, Pro-C3, High sensitivity CRP, Apo B and Apo C3, Leptin, Cytokeratin 18 appear in no panel and no body section (Section 3 mentions only 'mechanistic biomarkers', p 24); Luteinizing hormone (LH) is not in the Appendix 2 table (only FSH and estradiol under Hormones (Female)); Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) is not in the Appendix 2 table, though Section 8.1 says it is determined by the central laboratory; HIV, HCV and HBV screening tests are not in Appendix 2 — exclusion criteria 33-35 (p 37) name HIV antibodies, hepatitis B surface antigen, HCV RNA and anti-HCV but state no lab or specimen, so no lab_and_purpose entry was made; 'Pancreatic amylase' is listed in the Clinical Chemistry panel only as 'Amylase' (p 85), so no lab_and_purpose entry was made for it. The SoA row 'Symptom-directed physical examination' is not named in Section 8.2.1, which describes a 'brief physical examination' (p 56); the brief examination is recorded with no SoA activity. Lab specimens are not stated per panel in Appendix 2; Urinalysis and Urine Chemistry specimen left null. Hypersensitivity tests (pp 86-87) are event-driven and have no SoA row. Tokens in SoA activity names that no abbreviation list defines: LH, HBV, Pro-C3, CRP (defined only as clinical research physician), Apo B, Apo C3; the SoA row 'IWQOL-Lite CT' is spelled 'IWQOL-Lite- CT' in the SoA abbreviation list and 'IWQOL-Lite-CT' in Appendix 12; 'SF-36v2' is 'SF-36 v2 acute form' in Appendix 12. Instrument versions, modes and recall periods are recorded only where printed; IWQOL-Lite-CT, C-SSRS and Participant Survey state no recall period or mode in the body. Page 118 is an electronic approval stamp page, not protocol content. Updated 2026-11-07 to schema 1.1 (no re-extraction): instruments carry kind.
