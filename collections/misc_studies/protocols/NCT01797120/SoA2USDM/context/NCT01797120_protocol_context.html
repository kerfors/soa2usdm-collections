Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readProtocol version (rule 11): the PDF holds PrECOG PrE0102 Version 2.0 (January 22, 2014). PDF pages 1-17 are the Version 1.0 -> 2.0 revision history (Was/Is), pages 18-115 the Version 2.0 protocol. The SoA (Section 7 Study Parameters, pages 69-71) and its notes come from Version 2.0, and everything here is extracted from Version 2.0. No later amendment is in the PDF. The Was/Is pages show what Version 2.0 changed for SoA activities: Bicarbonate removed from the chemistry row (page 11; also 7.1.1-7.1.3 on pages 13-14), the 'HBV DNA, HCV RNA-PCR' row split into 'HBV DNA' and 'HCV RNA-PCR' (page 11), footnotes c and e changed from '≤ 72 hour' to '+/- 72 hour' (page 12), old footnote f split into f, g, h (page 12), old footnote g (imaging) became i without 'first assessment within 2 weeks prior to Cycle 4 treatment' (page 13). These Version 1.0 values are not recorded as entries. Conflicts (rule 5): (1) the revision history 'Is' text of footnote f on page 12 reads 'patients positive for HBV-DNA or HBsAg or HBsAb are treated prophylactically', while footnote f in the Version 2.0 SoA (page 70) and Section 5.3.4 (page 57) say 'HBV-DNA or HBsAg'. (2) HBV-DNA monitoring frequency: SoA footnote g and 7.1.2.1/7.1.2.3 give every cycle (C1D1 and Day 1 of all subsequent cycles, every 28 days), Table 5-3 (page 51) says 'Monitor HBV-DNA approximately every 4-8 weeks' / 'every 3-4 weeks'; HCV: SoA footnote h gives every cycle, 5.3.2.3 (page 52) says every 4-8 weeks. Both recorded in lab_and_purpose evidence. Panels: the protocol has no lab-tests table, so rule 10 did not apply and no page was read from a page image; all pages have a text layer. Panels are taken from the analyte lists in 7.1.1 (page 72). No panel heading is printed for the serum creatinine/electrolytes row; the panel name 'chemistry' is the word SoA note k (page 71) uses for it ('CBC and chemistry'). CBC components are not listed beyond 'with differential and platelets'. Urinalysis and PT with INR have no listed components. No central vs local lab is stated for any SoA lab activity; specimen is stated only where 'serum' is printed (fasting serum glucose page 55, Fasting Serum Lipid Profile). Inclusion criterion 11 (page 39) gives eligibility thresholds for WBC, ANC, platelets, hemoglobin, bilirubin, AST/ALT, creatinine, albumin, fasting cholesterol and triglycerides; only PT with INR is linked by name to an SoA row. Instruments/measurements: Physical Exam components are not described. 'Pulmonary function testing' (Table 5-6, page 56) is not linked to the SoA row 'PFTs with DLCO as medically indicated' because the protocol never expands 'PFTs'. NYHA classification is used only in exclusion criteria (no SoA row). RECIST 1.1 is recorded as the method of tumor measurements, not as an instrument. 'Ht' (SoA cell '(includes Ht)', page 69) is undefined; 7.1.1 lists 'height'. Abbreviations: the list on pages 93-97 has 'FSH' twice (page 94), both kept; misspellings ('Hpatitis', 'Lypoproteins', 'P13K') kept as printed. C1D1 is defined in SoA note a (page 70). Tokens in SoA activity names not defined in the abbreviation list: ECOG (only 'Source: Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group' on page 103), PT (list has only 'PT/PTT'), BUN (expanded only in body text page 50, 'blood urea nitrogen (BUN)'), HDL, PFTs, Meds (in 'Concomitant Meds'); SGOT and SGPT are only defined as part of 'AST/SGOT' and 'ALT/SGPT'. SoA rows the text does not further describe: Eligibility Assessment, Informed Consent, Medical History & Assessment of Baseline Signs and Symptoms, Physical Exam, Urinalysis (only listed in 7.1.1).
