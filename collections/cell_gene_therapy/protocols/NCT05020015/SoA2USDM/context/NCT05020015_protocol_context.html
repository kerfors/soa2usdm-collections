(4) Infectious serology: SoA row and p66 say HIV; screening list p59 says HIV-1. (5) Table footnote definitions differing from the main abbreviation list (recorded as extra entries): Cmax 'last measurable concentration' (p45) vs 'maximum observed concentration'; tlast '...above the lower limit of normal' (p45) vs main-list Tlast 'time of last measurable concentration' and endpoint text 'lower limit of quantitation'; ORR 'objective response rate' (p45) vs 'overall response rate'; PD 'pharmacodynamics' (p69) vs 'progressive disease'; HLA plural 'human leukocyte antigens' in SoA Table A 2 (p103); ULN 'upper limit of normal' in SoA (p100) vs 'upper limit of the normal range'. (6) RCR discontinuation rule differs: p63 'negative for the first year or after 2 negative consecutive tests (which ever comes later)' vs p72/SoA footnote f 'If all post-treatment assays ... are negative during the first year'. Table 6.b gives RCR time frame 'Up to 60 a months' (p45) while LTFU sampling runs to 15 years. (7) Section 9.4.19.5 (p73) points to Table A 1 and Table A 3 for immunogenicity samples, but the immunogenicity rows are in Table A 2 (p102) and Table A 3 (p104, humoral only, M12); cellular immunogenicity has no Table A 3 row. Table 8.a (p54) refers to Section 9.4.19.1 for clonality monitoring, but 9.4.19.1 is HLA and KIR typing. (8) Number of patients: 'Approximately 42' (p20, p43) vs '45 as of Amendment 5' in the Amendment 5 summary of changes (p6). Cross-references: all three SoA notes point to Section 10.3 (heading p77, content p78). Section 10.3 does not separate secondary follow-up from LTFU; it gives one rule set 'After Month 6 until EOS'. p. 32 TAK-007 is a cryopreserved allogeneic cellular product comprising human umbilical cord blood-derived natural killer (CB-NK) cells genetically modified with retroviral vector SFG.iC9.2A.CAR.CD19-28-3zeta.2A.IL15 encoding the transgenes for anti-CD19 chimeric antigen receptor (CAR), the CD28 endodomain, the ζ-chain of the T-cell receptor (TCR) complex, interleukin-15 (IL-15), and inducible caspase 9 (iCas9)-based safety switch.
p. 33 TAK-007 uses the genetically identical CAR construct transduced with the same viral vector used in the MDACC CD19 CAR+ NK study (Clinical Protocol 2016-0641) but differs in that it uses a semi-closed and scalable manufacturing process and is developed as a cryoformulation.
p. 36 TAK-007 (allogeneic SFG.iCas9.2A.CAR.CD19-28-3zeta.2A.IL15-transduced CB-NK cells with cryopreservation)
p. 56 TAK-007 is an unrelated, HLA-agnostic (not selected based on recipient HLA genotype), allogeneic donor umbilical CB-derived SFG.iCas9.2A.CAR.CD19-28-3zeta.2A.IL15 transduced NK cell product.
p. 50 The TAK-007 investigational product will be provided as a cryopreserved suspension of living cells that will be thawed by clinical staff immediately before administration. The doses of TAK-007 are 200 × 106 (±30%) and 800 × 106 (±25%) CD19 CAR+ viable NK cells per patient.
p. 35 TAK-007-2001 is the only study to date of the TAK-007 cryopreserved allogeneic CAR-NK cell therapy in humans that uses CB-derived NK cells.
