p. 1 AMT-061 (AAV5-hFIXco-Padua; etranacogene dezaparvovec)
p. 54 The IMP is identified as AAV5-hFIXco-Padua (hereafter referred as AMT-061; etranacogene dezaparvovec). AMT-061 is a recombinant adeno-associated viral vector of serotype 5 (AAV5) containing the Padua variant of a codon-optimized human factor IX cDNA under the control of a liver-specific promoter.
p. 54 The pharmaceutical form of AMT-061 is a solution for IV infusion.
p. 54 Subjects will receive a single IV infusion of 2 x 1013 gc/kg AMT-061.
p. 52 Since this is a gene therapy trial in which the IMP is administered to human subjects as a one-time only dose
p. 29 The AMT-060 and AMT-061 based gene therapy vectors are identical with the exception of a two-nucleotide substitution resulting in a single codon change (AGG to CTG) in the coding sequence for factor IX, corresponding to an Arginine to Leucine substitution in the transgenic protein.
p. 29 The R338L substitution, FIX-Padua, represents a naturally occurring variant of factor IX
p. 28 Both AMT-060 and AMT-061 employ the same AAV5 capsid as vector and liver-specific promoter, conferring similar safety and expression profiles.
