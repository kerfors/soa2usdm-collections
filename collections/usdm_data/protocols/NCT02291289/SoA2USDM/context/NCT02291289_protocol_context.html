Conflict: whole blood and plasma biomarker samples go to 'a Central Laboratory' in Sections 4.5.1.8 and 4.5.2.10 but to 'a designated laboratory' in the SoA notes; both recorded. Urinalysis: only the dipstick method is stated, no analytes listed (panel analytes empty); its bullet continues the local-laboratory list from page 113 onto page 114. Magnesium/amylase/lipase (Cohort 1 exp. arm) and magnesium/amylase/CPK/lipase (Cohort 4 exp. arm) are listed under sub-headings without a lab statement (lab null). Pregnancy test: SoA note for Cohort 3 adds serum pregnancy test ≤ 7 days from maintenance start and urine tests every 3 cycles and every 3 months until 7 months after the last trastuzumab/pertuzumab doses. Instruments: only ECOG performance status; RECIST 1.1 and mRECIST are recorded as the method of tumour assessments, not as instruments. No questionnaires, diaries or PROs. Measurements: no units, no ECG position/method beyond '12-lead' are stated. SoA activities with no protocol description beyond the name or administrative: Informed consent, Confirmation of general eligibility, Cohort-specific informed consent, Confirmation of cohort-specific eligibility, Assignment of cohort, Randomisation, Demographics and medical history, Concomitant medications, Adverse events (including SAEs), Study medication administration, Subsequent anti-cancer therapies, Patient survival (not lab/instrument/measurement items). All tokens in SoA activity names (INR, aPTT, SAEs/SAE, SCC, CT, ECG, TSH, T3, T4, HIV, HBV, HCV, LVEF, ECOG) are defined in the abbreviation list (pages 42-44); 'Tuberculosis test' appears in the body as 'TB test'. Metastatic tumour tissue and stool sample collection was discontinued as of May 2018 (Appendix 18); data collection after May 31, 2019 is governed by Appendix 19.
