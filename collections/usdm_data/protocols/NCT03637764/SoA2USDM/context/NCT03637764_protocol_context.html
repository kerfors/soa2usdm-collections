Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readProtocol version (rule 11): the PDF holds a single protocol version, Amended Clinical Trial Protocol 05 (ACT15377, 23-Nov-2020); the SoA and its notes (PDF pages 18-24) are from this version. Appendix 10 (pages 100-107) only summarises earlier amendments 01-04; no later amendment is included, so no additional amendment entries were recorded. Amendment history items touching SoA activities (e.g. Amendment 04: pregnancy test extended to monthly until 5 months after last dose) are already reflected in the current SoA and Table 10. Page images (rule 10): the lab-tests table (Table 10, page 93) was checked against the PDF page image; the text layer keeps each panel's analytes together in reading order (Hematology two columns, Clinical chemistry four columns), so panel assignments match the image. No page reads "[no text layer on this page]". Pages 123-124 (Appendix 19, CD38 blood test interference guideline AABB2016) carry only an embedded image of an external AABB bulletin and no protocol text; page 123 was viewed but nothing was taken from it. Cross-references: the SoA note "See Section 10.3 Table 12" (Blood Chemistry) gives two entries. Section 10.3 holds the lab-tests table, which is numbered Table 10; Table 12 in this document is "Table 12 - Response criteria" (RECIST, page 111), which does not list blood chemistry. Both are recorded as printed. Other internal reference conflicts: Section 8.2.4 (page 69) points to "Appendix 2 (Section 10.3)" and page 70 to "Appendix 2 (Section 10.2)" for laboratory assessments, while 10.3 is headed Appendix 3. The SoA notes "See Pharmacokinetics and immunogenicity Flow Chart" and "See Biomarker Flow Chart" point to the flow charts on pages 22-24, which are themselves part of the SoA input, so they are not in soa_cross_references. Labs: page 92 states the Table 10 tests are performed by the local laboratory; this is applied to the panels and to the SoA lab rows the SoA notes link to Section 10.3. Specimen is not stated for hematology, chemistry, coagulation, blood typing interference test or serology. The pregnancy test reads serum in the SoA note (page 19, within 7 days prior to first IMP administration) and "Serum (at baseline) or urine" in Table 10 (page 93, urine on Day 1 of each cycle, EOT and every 30±7 days); both are recorded. PK and ADA bioanalytical details are deferred to a separate Laboratory Manual (pages 74-75, 77); no central/local lab is stated for PK, ADA or biomarker samples. Instruments: only the ECOG Performance Status Scale and the Karnofsky Performance Scale (Appendix 14, page 117) are named for an SoA activity; no respondent, recall period or mode is printed. No questionnaires, PROs or diaries are used. Response criteria (RECIST 1.1, RANO, iRECIST, iRANO, GCIG CA-125) are tumour-assessment criteria reached via the Section 8.1 cross-reference and are not recorded as instruments; BCLC staging and Child Pugh score (Appendices 12-13) are eligibility classifications not tied to an SoA row. Measurements: CT/MRI and Brain MRI disease assessments are covered by the Section 8.1 cross-reference, not as measurements. No units are printed for any measurement. The pulse-oximetry method for "Resting O2 saturation for SCCHN" comes from inclusion criterion I 12 (page 48). SoA activity names: tokens not defined in the abbreviation list or SoA abbreviation lines: CA125 (written CA-125 in Appendix 17, not expanded), CT, DNA, O2, PS. "Atezolizumab" and "Isatuximab" each occur twice in soa_activities (IMP administration rows and Immunogenicity (ADA) rows of the PK/immunogenicity flow chart) and are not given separate entries; their PK/ADA details sit under "PK", "ADA", "Atezolizumab Sample ID" and "Isatuximab Sample ID". The SoA notes T2:a, T2:e and T2:i are bound to "Pharmacokinetics" and "Immunogenicity (ADA)", which are flow-chart section headers and not in soa_activities. "Core 1", "Core 2" and "Core 3" are tumour-core labels with no separate text description. No text describes "Demography, Medical/Surgical and Disease History", "Subsequent Anticancer Therapy Status" or "Survival Status" beyond the SoA rows (outside the categories). The abbreviation list prints PD-L1 twice (pages 99 and 100) and SAE twice (page 100); each printed line is kept. The definitions of SGOT and SGPT keep the text layer's line-break hyphen ("glutamic- oxaloacetic", "glutamic- pyruvic"), and FUP on page 23 reads "follow- up period".
