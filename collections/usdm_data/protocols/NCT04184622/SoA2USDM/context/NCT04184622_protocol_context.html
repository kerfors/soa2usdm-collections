Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readProtocol version: the PDF holds one protocol version, Protocol I8F-MC-GPHK(b) (Amendment (b), approved 04-Oct-2021); the SoA (pp 18-29) and its notes come from this version. Appendix 12 (pp 126-127) only summarises Amendment (a) changes already incorporated (e.g. waist circumference added to Visit 199, 'register study visit in IWRS' added, Visit 21 window); no later amendment in the PDF changes an SoA activity, so no additional entries were made. No page reads '[no text layer on this page]'; no item is read_from page image. Rule 10: the Appendix 2 Clinical Laboratory Tests table (p 96) runs its two columns together in the text layer and was checked on the page image: Total Cholesterol, Triglycerides and Creatine kinase (CK) belong to Clinical Chemistry (the text layer places them after Urine Chemistries), Urine Chemistries holds only Albumin and Creatinine, Lipid Panel holds LDL-C, HDL-C, VLDL-C, and Insulin / C-peptide are separate headings. The Appendix 5 Hepatic Monitoring Tests table (p 109) was also checked on its page image for the panel assignment. The Hypersensitivity Tests table (p 98) is one panel and was assigned from the text layer alone. Text-layer superscripts are merged into words (e.g. 'Insuline' = Insulin with footnote e, 'Hematologya' = Hematology with footnote a); analytes are written without the footnote letters, quotes keep the text layer. Lab: p 95 states all Appendix 2 tests are performed by the central laboratory unless otherwise stated, and footnote a names a Lilly-designated central laboratory; local laboratory results are only required when central results are not available in time. Hepatic monitoring tests (p 109) are assayed by a Lilly-designated or local laboratory. Immunogenicity is assessed at a laboratory approved by the Sponsor. Notable: the SoA 'Lipid panel' row corresponds to the Lipid Panel heading (LDL-C, HDL-C, VLDL-C) while Total Cholesterol and Triglycerides are listed under Clinical Chemistry. SoA activity name differences from the notes/text: 'Chemistry panel (include Cr for eGFR calculation and glucose)' vs note heading 'Chemistry panel (include Cr for eGFR calculation)' (p 29); 'Urinary albumin/creatinine' vs 'Urinary albumin/creatinine ratio' (p 29); 'C-SSRS (Baseline/Screening) Version)' has an extra parenthesis as printed in the SoA (p 22); 'IWQOL-Lite CT' vs 'IWQOL-Lite-CT'. Abbreviations: the Appendix 11 list (pp 121-125) is given in full, plus abbreviations defined only under the SoA tables (pp 25, 29) and lab-tests tables (pp 97, 98, 109). Conflicting/variant definitions recorded as printed: CK = 'creatinine kinase' (p 97) for Creatine kinase; UACR = 'urine albumin/creatinine ration' (p 97); TEE = 'total energy exposure' (p 125) while p 55 uses 'total energy expenditure (TEE)'; IWQOL-Lite-CT definition differs slightly between p 25 and p 123; SF-36 (p 25) vs SF-36v2 (p 124); IWRS is 'interactive web-response system' in the list but 'interactive web response service' under tables on pp 57 and 64 (not SoA/lab tables, not added). Tokens in SoA activity names not defined in the abbreviation list: Cr, TZP and PK are defined only under the SoA tables (p 25/29); SF-36 is defined under the SoA table (the list has SF-36v2); 'EQ-5D-5L' is not in any abbreviation list (named in Section 8.1.6); 'c-peptide' and 'Cystatin-c' are not abbreviations. Instruments: mode 'paper source document' comes from Section 10.1.7 (clinical outcome assessment data — scales, self-reported diary data — collected via a paper source document and transcribed into EDC). Version, recall period and respondent are null where not printed (PHQ-9 recall period, C-SSRS rater, Self-Harm forms content are not described). PRO administration order (PGIS, SF-36v2, IWQOL-Lite-CT, EQ-5D-5L) is stated on p 39. SoA rows the text does not describe beyond the SoA itself: Informed consent, Randomization, Register study visit in IWRS, Adverse events and product complaints, Concomitant medications, Dispense study drug, Participant returns study drugs and injection supplies, Injection training with autoinjector demonstration device and Observe participant administer study drug (described only procedurally, p 39), Lifestyle Program instructions / Review diet and exercise goals (counselling content on pp 27-28 and 55, not measurements). The Nonpharmacogenetic stored sample row is given its specimens from the lab table (Serum, EDTA plasma, P800 plasma); Section 8.8 (Biomarkers) states serum and plasma samples for biomarker research are collected per the SoA but does not name this row, so no purpose is recorded. Section 8.6 states pharmacodynamic samples are included in the efficacy measures. Updated 2026-11-07 to schema 1.1 (no re-extraction): instruments carry kind.
