RT-11 vital-sign measurement in a vital-signs panel: VS-OV → VS
VS-OV names blood pressure, temperature, respiration, body surface area, body mass index, height and weight as measurements of the VS domain. A member of a panel whose label names vital signs, and which names one of them (BP, SBP, DBP, temp, RR, BSA and BMI read as the same), is a vital-signs measurement. Only within such a panel: outside it, weight and temperature keep their test identity. Pulse and heart rate are not named in VS-OV and stay with their test identity (CV or VS).
