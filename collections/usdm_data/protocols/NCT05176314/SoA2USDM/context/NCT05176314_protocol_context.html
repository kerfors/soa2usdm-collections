Panels 9-16 are the Appendix 6 liver-safety follow-up assessments (selected per Section 8.2.7.1, hepatic monitoring), analysed by the Lilly-designated central laboratory except Microbiology (investigator-designated local laboratory only) — a different lab from Appendix 2; both recorded. Panel names Hematology, Clinical Chemistry and Serology occur in both tables. Specimens for the panels are not stated except for the pregnancy test. Abbreviations: Appendix 7 list (pages 60-61, including glossary terms; 'investigation al product' is kept as split in the text layer), the SoA abbreviation line (page 13), the Appendix 2 table (page 46) and parenthetical definitions in the Appendix 6 table (pages 58-59, 'Hepatis B virus' as printed). Text describes procedures not visible as SoA rows: pirtobrutinib PK samples (Section 8.4 and the Blood Sampling Summary, page 47: 15 samples) and a separate blood sample for storage for potential pharmacogenetic analysis (Section 8.6; page 47: 'Pharmacogenetics for storage'); these may be the redacted 'CCI' rows.
