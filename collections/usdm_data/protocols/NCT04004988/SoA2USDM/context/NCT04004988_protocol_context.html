Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readSingle protocol version in the PDF (Protocol I8F-MC-GPGS, approved 04-Jun-2019); no amendments, so rule 11 did not apply. Rule 10: the Appendix 2 lab table (PDF page 46) and the Appendix 4 hepatic monitoring table (PDF page 50) run two columns together in the text layer; both were read from the page images before assigning analytes to panels (Hematology vs Clinical Chemistry; Urinalysis vs the unheaded list Hepatitis B surface antigen / Hepatitis C antibody / HIV or HIV antibody / FSH / Pregnancy test; Hepatic Hematology / Chemistry vs Coagulation / Serologies and the four single tests). The image also confirmed that the Neutrophils-Basophils rows sit under 'Absolute counts of:' in Hematology. The five tests in the right column below Clinical Chemistry have no sub-heading in the table, so they are recorded under the table heading 'Safety Laboratory Tests' (split by footnote: screening-only serology c; FSH and pregnancy test d). Triglyceride carries footnote c (local laboratory at screening only) although listed under Clinical Chemistry. No page lacks a text layer except PDF page 43, which is the Appendix 1 heading page and is blank in the image too (the abbreviation list is on pages 44-45); the Appendix 7 algorithm figure (page 54) and the study design figure (page 16) were not needed. Abbreviations: Appendix 1 list (pages 44-45) as printed, including the defined non-abbreviation terms, plus the abbreviation lines under the SoA (page 12), the Appendix 2 lab table (page 46) and the Appendix 4 table (page 50); 'Non- investigational product (non-IP)' keeps the text-layer hyphen break. All tokens in SoA activity names (CRU, ECG, FSH, AEs, PK) are defined (SoA abbreviation line or lab-table line). Lab statements recorded side by side: PK and immunogenicity samples are analysed 'at a laboratory approved by the sponsor' (sections 9.5.1, 9.7) and listed as 'central or referral laboratory' in Appendix 5. Pregnancy test specimen: serum at screening, urine at subsequent visits (SoA note and Appendix 2 footnote d agree). Specimen for Clinical Chemistry is not stated for the panel; only amylase and lipase are called serum (section 9.4.1.1). Instruments: the protocol names no questionnaire, rating scale, diary or performance status, so the list is empty. SoA rows the text does not describe beyond the SoA itself: Informed Consent (Appendix 3 only covers the ICF process), Subject Admission to CRU, Tirzepatide Dosing (section 7 covers the dose, not an assessment), Subject Discharge from CRU, Outpatient Visit, Medical History and Demographics, Weight, Height, Temperature (no components, position or units printed; weight/height appear only as demographic characteristics in section 10.2.2), AEs/Concomitant Medications (sections 9.2 and 7.7). Not recorded as panels because they are not in a lab-tests table: unscheduled immune safety laboratory testing after hypersensitivity reactions ('including, but not limited to β tryptase, total IgE, complement and cytokine panel testing', page 34) and the injection-site reaction categories (edema, erythema, induration, itching, pain; page 33), which are not an SoA row.
