Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readProtocol version: the PDF holds only Protocol WA42380 Version 3 (approval stamp 11-Jun-2020); earlier versions 1 (18 March 2020) and 2 (14 April 2020) are listed in the protocol history (p2) but not included, and the SoA (Appendices 1-3, pp77-85) belongs to Version 3. No separate amendment changes to record. Rule 10: there is no columnar lab-tests table; lab tests are bulleted lists in Section 4.5.6 (pp39-40) and SoA notes, so no page needed image checking for panel assignment. Page 77 and 86 were rendered as images: page 86 (Appendix 4) carries the NEWS2 scoring table as an embedded image not present in the text layer (parameters respiration rate, SpO2 Scale 1/2, air or oxygen, systolic blood pressure, pulse, consciousness, temperature, each scored 0-3); it is not quoted, only referred to in the NEWS2 scoring_or_range. No page lacks a text layer. The text layer renders some Symbol-font glyphs as private-use characters (U+F0A3 for ≤, U+F0E2 for ®) and drops others (e.g. '04 hours' for 0–4 hours); quotes keep the text-layer form, field values use the printed symbol. Conflicts: (1) AE reporting period - SoA note j says all adverse events reported 'until 60 days after the final dose of study drug', Section 5.6 (p60) defines the period as '60 days after study initiation', Section 5.3.1 (p49) says 'during the 60-day follow-up period'. (2) Hematology lymphocyte subsets carry 'if the test is available at the site' in Section 4.5.6 and Appendix 1/2 notes, but not in Appendix 3 note g (p85). (3) Complete physical examination: Section 4.5.3 says 'performed at screening'; SoA note c adds 'and per the investigator’s discretion during the study'. (4) SpO2 is defined three ways: 'blood oxygen saturation' (p19), 'peripheral capillary oxygen saturation' (SoA, p78/82/84), 'oxygen saturation' (p86). NEWS2 = 'National Early Warning Score 2' (p18) vs 'National Early Warning Score' (p78). The abbreviation list spells 'sIL6-R'; the SoA and body use 'sIL-6R'. (5) Chemistry analyte order differs (4.5.6 'D-dimer, and ferritin'; SoA 'ferritin, and D-dimer'). Labs: Section 4.5.6 states local lab for PaO2, hematology, chemistry, pregnancy test and SARS-CoV-2 PCR; designated central laboratories/Sponsor for PK, PD, exploratory biomarkers, antibody titer, viral load swabs/BAL, PAXgene RNA and PBMCs (matching the SoA 'Central Labs' header). Section 9.5 (p74) says accredited local laboratories are used for routine monitoring. The 7-category ordinal scale is also used in SoA note n (Study drug administration) as the criterion for a second infusion; it is linked only to 'Ordinal scoring'. No PRO questionnaires or diaries are used; PRO-CTCAE is defined in the SoA abbreviations but never used. Abbreviations: the list (pp18-19) plus the abbreviations printed under the SoA tables (p78; p82 adds BAL; p84 repeats CRP, CT, PK, SpO2 and is not repeated here) and under the NEWS2 table (p86). Tokens in SoA activity names not defined in the abbreviation list: 'PD' (Serum PD - not defined anywhere), 'RNA', 'SARS-CoV-2' (list has only SARS-CoV; body p20 names SARS-CoV-2 as the novel coronavirus strain), 'COVID-19' (explained in body p20 only), 'sIL-6R' (list spells sIL6-R), 'FiO2' (only as part of PaO2/FiO2 in SoA abbreviations; body p38 'fraction of inspired oxygen (FiO2)'), 'PBMCs', 'CT' and 'ECG' (SoA abbreviations only), 'X-ray', 'PAXgene' (trade name). SoA rows the text describes only in general terms or not at all as an assessment: Informed consent, Inclusion/exclusion criteria, Demographic data (age, sex, self-reported race/ethnicity, p38), Randomization, Medical history, Concomitant medications, Study drug administration - not measurements/instruments, so not in these categories. Serum SARS-CoV-2 antibody titer has no stated method or purpose. Updated 2026-11-07 to schema 1.1 (no re-extraction): instruments carry kind.
