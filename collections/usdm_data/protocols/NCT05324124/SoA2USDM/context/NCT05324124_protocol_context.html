Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readSingle protocol version in the PDF: the initial protocol J2G-MC-JZPA (title page p2: 'This is the initial protocol.'), approved 22-Nov-2021; no amendments, so rule 11 did not apply. Instruments: the protocol names no questionnaire, rating scale, diary or performance-status instrument for any SoA activity; list empty. Page images (rule 10): p48 (Safety Laboratory Tests) whose text layer runs the Hematology and Clinical Chemistry columns together - the image confirmed the assignments: Differential WBC (absolute counts) is a sub-heading in the hematology column, Coagulation (footnote a: screening only) sits in the left column, and the six single tests (Hepatitis B surface antigen, Hepatitis C antibody, HIV, Pregnancy test, FSH, Hepatitis C RNA) stand in the right column under no heading, so each is recorded as its own entry named as printed. pp61-62 (Appendix 6 Hepatic Evaluation Testing, conditional liver-safety follow-up, not an SoA row) were also read from images: two-column table whose text layer interleaves the columns; panels recorded as printed (Hematology, Coagulation, Serology, Microbiology on the left; Clinical Chemistry, Other Chemistry, Urine Chemistry, Other Serology on the right), lab = Lilly-designated central laboratory except Microbiology (investigator-designated local laboratory only). The panel names Hematology, Clinical Chemistry and Coagulation therefore appear twice: once for Appendix 2 (p48, local laboratory) and once for Appendix 6 (pp61-62). 'Hepatis B/C virus' is the protocol's spelling. p13 (last SoA page) and p49 (Blood Sampling Summary) have a letter-spaced text layer ('C R U = cli nical researc h u nit') that no verbatim quote can be matched against in readable form; the p13 SoA abbreviation line (CRU, ECG, ED, h) was read from the page image and marked read_from 'page image' although the page does have a (garbled) text layer. p49 was not used. Conflicts (rule 5): p47 states the Appendix 2 tests 'will be performed by the local laboratory' while the next bullet speaks of the sponsor approving local testing 'in lieu of central laboratory testing'; both recorded under Clinical laboratory tests. Pregnancy test: SoA note and p48 footnote f say serum at screening and urine at all other time points, whereas the Appendix 4 contraception table (p57) says 'Negative serum result at screening followed by a negative serum result within 24 hours prior to treatment exposure'; both recorded as separate lab_and_purpose entries. The SoA has a row block redacted as 'CCI' (also in soa_activities and in the SoA abbreviation line, which is cut off after 'h = hour;' by a CCI redaction); CCI is a redaction marker, not an abbreviation defined in the protocol. The SoA cell token 'P' (e.g. 'P, 2h') is not defined in the visible SoA abbreviation line or the Appendix 8 list. No pharmacokinetic sampling row is visible in the SoA although Section 8.4 describes plasma PK samples (presumably in the redacted CCI block; not recorded). SoA rows the text does not further describe: Informed consent (process only), Inclusion and exclusion criteria, Demography, Participant admission to CRU, Participant discharge from CRU, Outpatient visit, Medical history, Adverse event /Serious adverse event review, Concomitant medication review, Selpercatinib administration (dosing described in Section 6.1.1, not a measurement). Vital signs: the protocol lists only blood pressure and pulse rate (no temperature or respiratory rate). ECG: the protocol does not list ECG parameters as components (QTc/QTcB/QTcF appear only in stopping and exclusion criteria), so components is empty. Height and weight are also stated as part of the complete medical assessment (Section 8.2.1). Units of measurement are not printed for any measurement. Clinical laboratory tests: specimen not stated for the Appendix 2 panels (urine for urinalysis and serum/urine for pregnancy only by name/footnote). Updated 2026-11-07 to schema 1.1 (no re-extraction): instruments carry kind; p13 abbreviations no longer marked page image (rule 12).
