Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readProtocol version: the PDF (CTR appendix 16.1.1) holds the original protocol version 2.0 (21 December 2017; PDF pages 3-106, Attachments I/II note p107) followed by amendment 2 (UK, pp108-110), amendment 3 (global, pp111-118), amendment 4 (DE, pp119-126), amendment 5 (UK, pp127-134) and amendment 6 (global, to protocol version 3.0, pp135-155). The SoA and its footnotes are printed on pp10-15 of version 2.0, so all categories are extracted from version 2.0; later changes are additional entries (rule 11): amendment 3 - biosamples for future analysis become whole blood (genetic) + serum, SoA row reference changes to (9.7, 9.8, Appendix 7) [lab_and_purpose]; amendment 6 - anti-semaglutide antibody positive samples also titrated [lab_and_purpose]; extension-phase lab table (V25ext-V28ext: HbA1C, lipids, hsCRP, all non-fasting) [last three panels]; abbreviations CTRext, ExAS, EXT added (p155). Amendment changes not captured as entries: amendment 6 changes SoA footnote b to 'For all female subjects of child bearing potential', adds Concomitant medication at V25, corrects the Tobacco Use reference from 6.4.1 to 6.4.2 (p139), and adds an extension-phase flowchart (p149: informed consent, inclusion/exclusion, concomitant medication, body measurements, vital signs, laboratory assessment); amendment 3 changes the Table 11-3 lymphocyte outlier cut-off (<25 to <0.2 x 10^9/L). Conflicts recorded: pregnancy test - Table 11-2 tests are stated to be performed by the central laboratory and 9.4.5 says urine tests are supplied by the central laboratory, while note 5 says local urine testing is standard unless serum testing is required locally. Fasting at V25: SoA footnote g says at least 2 hours without food and drink except water; 6.4.1 says at least 8 hours overnight generally, 2 hours at end of trial before anti-semaglutide antibody sampling. Cross-reference oddities (recorded as printed): 'Tobacco Use (6.4.1)' - tobacco is in 6.4.2 Caffeine and tobacco; 'Technical complaint (9.2.9, Appendix 4)' - technical complaint definitions are in Appendix 6 (9.2.9 says so), Appendix 4 is adverse events; 'Informed consent and Demography (Appendix 3)' - Appendix 3 covers the informed consent process, not demography. The 'Section 9' entry carries several quotes because the SoA rows citing it (weight history, lipid-lowering/antihypertensive/glycaemic evaluations, DEXA, barriers interview, diaries) are described in the unnumbered body of Section 9 (pp37-39). Rule 10: lab-tests tables on pp68-69 (Tables 11-1, 11-2) were checked against the page images; the text layer lists each parameter on its own line under its group and the images confirmed the analyte-to-panel assignments. No page lacks a text layer, so nothing is read_from page image. Panel analytes drop footnote digits printed as superscripts (e.g. 'Fasting plasma glucose1', 'Amylase3'). Instruments: mode null for the PROs - Appendix 3 (p78) mentions 'electronic patient reported outcomes instruments' generally without naming which; no recall periods printed for any questionnaire. Abbreviations: complete Appendix 1 list (pp65-67, 72 lines) plus hCG and INR defined only in the lab-tests table (p69). Tokens in SoA activity names/notes not defined: 'ID' (Hand out ID card), 'V25' (footnote g); 'HbA1c' is listed as 'HbA1C'; 'dairy' in 'Hand out and instruct in PK dairy' is the printed spelling; PHQ-9 is spelled 'Patient Health Questionaire-9' in the list. SoA rows the text does not describe beyond the flowchart: Childbearing potential (only Appendix 5 'recorded in the CRF'), Evaluation of diet and physical activity, First date on trial product, IWRS session, Dispensing visit, Hand out ID card (Section 9 mentions a card with trial-site contact details given at screening). Measurements exclude medical history, tobacco use and demography (SoA footnote a: date of birth, sex, ethnicity, race). Updated 2026-11-07 to schema 1.1 (no re-extraction): instruments carry kind.
