No page reads '[no text layer on this page]'. PDF pages 136-143 have no text because they are fully redacted (black boxes, checked on the page image of p136); sections there could not be read. Several SoA notes and body passages also carry redactions (marked '[remainder redacted in source]' in the SoA inputs; gaps on pp 124 and 132). Rule 10: the lab-tests table (Table 9.4.6-1, pp 130-131) was checked on the page images; Chemistry is printed in two columns (AST..Glucose left, Albumin..Lipase right) and the text layer reads the left column then the right column, so the panel assignments match the image. Appendix 7 (p194) was also viewed: the top row value '100' (Normal, no complaints / Fully active, normal) is printed on the image but missing from the text layer; the Karnofsky/Lansky scoring_or_range 100-to-10 rests on the image for '100'. Cross-references: all 37 targets found. Section 9.1 (Efficacy Assessments) is cited by the CSF and bone-marrow notes; for CSF it says CSF analysis is included for RAPNO tumors (9.1.2.4); for bone marrow in solid tumors the readable text of 9.1 says nothing specific (only 9.1.2.5 for leukemia), so what_it_says is null for those entries. Response criteria (RECIST, INRC, IPNHLRC, RANO, RAPNO, NCCN, Cheson IWG) are recorded as instruments, linked to Body/Brain Imaging and CSF/bone-marrow rows through Section 9.1.2 (sub-sections of 9.1 cited by SoA notes). Conflicts/differences: vital signs components differ - Section 9.4.2 lists pulse rate, systolic and diastolic blood pressure and temperature (pulse oximetry at baseline only), SoA notes list blood pressure, heart rate and temperature (Table 2-2 adds weight; screening adds height, weight and pulse oximetry). PRO-CTCAE: SoA note says weekly in Cycles 1-3, every cycle Cycles 4-8, then every other cycle until end of treatment, and caregiver version for children <7 years; Section 9.1.4 says 'may be administered' with the same schedule plus at end of treatment, and adds the caregiver version for older participants with a cognitive impairment. Pregnancy test sensitivity wording varies between the three SoA tables and Table 9.4.6-1 (same 25 IU/L). The CTCAE abbreviation under Table 2-2 includes 'version 5' while Appendix 1 does not; FSH hyphenation differs between Appendix 1 and Table 9.4.6-1. Lab: the protocol does not state central vs local lab for the safety laboratory tests; only the follow-up phase is stated as on-site/local labs (p121), and Appendix 6 (CVA algorithm, p193) asks for coagulation/CBC/BUN/creatinine 'preferably by central laboratory testing' (not an SoA row, not recorded as a panel). No specimen is stated per panel except the pregnancy test (urine or serum); the 'Other Analyses' specimen value refers to the pregnancy test only. SoA activity name tokens: all abbreviations used in SoA activity names (SAE, ECG, MUGA, CSF, PK, PRO, CTCAE) are defined in Appendix 1. PRO-CTCAE, Ped-PRO-CTCAE and NCI-CTCAE are not in Appendix 1; they are added from p68 (objectives table), p124 and the SoA note on p32. The SoA input uses 'Serious Adverse Events (SAE) Assessment' for the Table 2-2 row printed as 'Adverse Events Assessment (including Serious Adverse Events)' and the Table 2-3 row 'Adverse Events and Serious Adverse Events (SAE) Assessment'. SoA note T2:e is bound to 'Pharmacokinetic (PK)/Immunogenicity Assessments', a section header not in soa_activities. SoA rows with little or no body text: Informed Consent, Medical History, Prior and Concomitant Medication Use, Subsequent Medications, Review of Subsequent Anti-cancer Therapy, Survival Status, Determine Dosing Schema, Administer Bempegaldesleukin + Nivolumab - described only in SoA notes or Sections 5/7, not as measurements or labs. Physical examination content is not described (Section 9.4.1 only refers to the SoA). Updated 2026-11-07 to schema 1.1 (no re-extraction): instruments carry kind.
