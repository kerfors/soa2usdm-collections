Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readProtocol version (rule 11): the PDF holds a single protocol version, I4T-MC-JVDU amendment (e) (approval 26-Feb-2021); the SoA pages (17-24) and the PK schedule (25) carry the running header 'Protocol I4T-MC-JVDU(e)', so all items come from amendment (e). Appendix 8 (pages 106-110) summarises earlier amendments a-d (e.g. amendment b added coagulation on C2D1 and every other cycle; amendment a clarified no Pain VAS for spontaneous ISRs) - these are already reflected in (e), so no additional entries were needed. Page images (rule 8): 27 pages have no text layer. Read from images: 17-25 (SoA, continued-access SoA, PK sampling schedule), 32 (objectives), 38 (inclusion criteria), 45 (Section 6.1 table), 86-87 (Appendix 2 lab tests), 88-90 (Appendix 3, incl. 10.3.3 Assessment of Intensity, the target of the SoA's 'Section 10.3' reference). All entries marked read_from 'page image' are unchecked by the quote checker; SoA-note quotes on page images are given with '...' where they join the row label to the note. Not read: 13-15 (synopsis), 39 (inclusion criteria cont.), 56 (dose-reduction table), 91-92 (SAE reporting), 97-98 (Appendix 5 liver safety follow-up tests), 100 (Appendix 6 table). Appendix 5 may list hepatic follow-up tests; only the Section 8.2.4 text list (page 67) is recorded. Rule 10: the lab-tests table (Appendix 2, pages 86-87) has no text layer at all, so every panel assignment rests on the page image; Hypersensitivity Tests (central laboratory) are listed there for the HSR samples. Conflicts / differences (rule 5): (1) Chemistry is central laboratory in Appendix 2, but Section 6.1.1 (page 47) says bilirubin, AST and ALT for dosing decisions are collected locally and centrally, and ANC/platelets locally; in the continued access period all laboratory tests are local only (page 24) - recorded as a second Clinical chemistry entry. (2) Pregnancy test: Appendix 2 says urine pregnancy test (serum if not confirmable as negative); the inclusion criterion (page 41) says urine or serum as required by local regulations. (3) ISR symptoms: erythema, induration, pain, pruritus and edema (pages 50, 69) vs pain, erythema, induration, swelling and pruritus (page 67) - recorded as two measurement entries. (4) CTCAE v5.0 for AE grading, but v4.03 for hepatic laboratory assessments (pages 8, 90). (5) Section 6.5.1.8.1 (page 51) gives HSR sample time points as onset, resolution and 30 days after onset; the continued-access SoA note says 30 (±3) days following the hypersensitivity. Abbreviations: Appendix 7 (pages 101-105) transcribed line by line (QW is listed twice, pages 104 and 105). Added abbreviations defined only under the SoA tables (pages 23, 24, 25) and in the lab-tests table (pages 86-87), all from page images. Undefined tokens in SoA activity names: 'IG' is not in Appendix 7; it is defined only under the SoA tables (IG = immunogenicity, pages 23-24) and on the objectives page. 'Sample 1'-'Sample 14' and 'End of treatment' are the rows of the PK sampling schedule (Section 1.3.1, page 25), not described individually in the text; Section 8.5/8.9 (PK, immunogenicity) apply to them via the PK and IG rows, so no separate lab_and_purpose entries were made. SoA rows the text does not describe beyond the SoA note: Physical examination (no components printed; mentioned only in safety review and hepatic evaluation), Informed consent, Inclusion/Exclusion criteria, Medical history, Cancer treatment history, Concomitant medication (not measurements; excluded by category rules). ECG: no ECG components or parameters are listed (QTcF appears only in the abbreviation list). Section 10.1.7 (page 84) says clinical outcome assessment data are collected by the participant via a paper source document but does not name the instrument, so mode was left null for the VAS and diary. Page 64: the CT slice-thickness recommendation contains a private-use glyph in the text layer (likely a less-or-equal sign before 5 mm) and was left out of the tumor-assessment method. Health economics, pharmacodynamics, genetics and biomarkers are stated as not evaluated (pages 73-74). Updated 2026-11-07 to schema 1.1 (no re-extraction): instruments carry kind.
