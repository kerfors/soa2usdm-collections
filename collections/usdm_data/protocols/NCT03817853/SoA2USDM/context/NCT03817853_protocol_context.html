Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readSingle protocol version in the PDF: Protocol MO40597, Version 5 (approval stamp 30-Jun-2021, p2); the protocol history (p3) lists versions 1-4 but only Version 5 is included, and the SoA (Appendix 1, pp101-103) is from it, so rule 11 adds no amendment entries. Pages 104-105 (Appendix 2, MDASI core items) have no text layer and were read from the page images: they give the MDASI 0-10 rating scales, the 'in the last 24 hours' recall and the 19 items; the two MDASI evidence entries from them are marked read_from 'page image'. Rule 10: no lab-tests table with columns; the local lab tests are a bulleted list (pp55-56) whose text layer reads cleanly, so no page needed image checking. All lab tests are local ('No samples in this study will be sent to a central laboratory'). Conflicts recorded: (1) chemistry analytes differ between Section 4.5.6 (p56: phosphorus, uric acid, alkaline phosphatase, beta-2 microglobulin unrestricted) and SoA note n (p103: phosphate, urate, ALP, beta-2 microglobulin at screening and EOI only) - recorded as two panels; (2) pregnancy test population/timing: p56 'not postmenopausal ... or surgically sterile ... within 7 days of Day 1 of Cycle 1' vs SoA note o (p103) 'women of childbearing potential ... at screening'; (3) vital signs: SoA note g says to record baseline abnormalities on the General Medical History and Baseline Conditions eCRF, while Section 4.5.4 (p55) says vital sign data do not need to be recorded on the eCRF except for adverse events; (4) Grade 3-4 infection reporting: SoA note w 'until resolution or until up to 2 years after the last dose of obinutuzumab' vs Section 5.6 'until up to 2 years after the last dose of study treatment'; (5) concomitant medication window: SoA note u 'until 3 months after the final dose of study drug' vs Section 4.4 (p51) 'to the end of the 3-month safety follow-up visit'; (6) HTLV-1 is 'human T-lymphotropic virus' in the abbreviation list (p24) but 'human T-lymphotropic virus 1' in the SoA legend (p101). Cross-references: the 'Table 1' cited in SoA notes c and d is Table 1 in Section 3.1 (p39); Appendix 5 also has tables titled 'Table 1' (pp110-111). Section 3.1 itself refers to Section 4.3.1 for cycle numbers while SoA note b refers to 4.3.2.2. Abbreviations: the full list (pp23-24) plus the SoA table legend (p101) and aPTT from the lab list (p56). Tokens in SoA activity names not in the abbreviation list: 'FLIPI' (defined only in the SoA legend p101 and inline on p54), 'FLIPI2' (never expanded as an abbreviation; Appendix 5 table title reads 'Follicular Lymphoma International Prognostic Index 2'), 'Hep B / C' ('Hep' undefined; list has HBV/HCV = hepatitis B/C virus), 'aPTT' and 'PTT' (list has '(a)PTT'; aPTT defined on p56). In SoA notes, 'ALP' and 'C1D1' are not defined anywhere. SoA rows the text does not describe beyond the SoA: Weight and Height (no section gives method or units; BSA is mentioned only for chemotherapy capping). Demographic data, Medical history, B symptoms, Informed consent, Concomitant medications and Adverse events are described in the text but are not measurements or instruments. Instruments: Ann Arbor, FLIPI and FLIPI2 are recorded as instruments (staging/prognostic index with printed scoring in Appendices 4-5); their type is not stated. No instrument versions are printed. Updated 2026-11-07 to schema 1.1 (no re-extraction): instruments carry kind.
