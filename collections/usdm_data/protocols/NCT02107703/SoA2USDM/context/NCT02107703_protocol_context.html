Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readSingle protocol version in the PDF: Protocol I3Y-MC-JPBL, approval 01-Apr-2014 (pages 1-93; page 93 is the electronic approval page). No amendments are included; the SoA (Attachment 1, pages 73-79) comes from this version. No page lacks a text layer. Rule 10: the Attachment 2 lab-tests table (page 80) and the Attachment 3 hepatic monitoring table (page 81) were checked on the page images, because the text layer interleaves the two columns (e.g. Sodium/Chloride appear after Hematocrit); panel assignments follow the images, quotes come from the text layer. On page 80, Serum Pregnancy Test, FSH level and Estradiol level sit in the chemistry column below Albumin but carry footnote b (local lab) instead of a (Lilly-designated), so they are recorded as separate entries. Attachment 3 hepatic monitoring tests are not an SoA row; they are conditional (in the event of a treatment-emergent hepatic abnormality). Conflicts: CTCAE version is 4.03 in the synopsis Safety criteria, Section 6.2 and Section 12.2.12 but 4.0 in the synopsis Objectives and Section 10.3.1; HR = hazard ratio in the abbreviation list but HR = heart rate under the SoA; FSH = follicle-stimulating hormone in the list and Attachment 2 but follicular stimulating hormone under the SoA; the SoA row says BPI and EORTC BR23 while the SoA note k and body say mBPI-sf and EORTC QLQ-BR23 (abbreviation list defines BPI-sf and EORTC BR23; synopsis defines BPI as Brief Pain Inventory; Section 12.2.11 defines mBPI-sf as modified Brief Pain Inventory, Short Form). Tokens in SoA activity names not in the main abbreviation list: Temp, BP, RR (defined only under the SoA), PK (defined only under the SoA and Attachment 7), BPI (list has BPI-sf only), HR (list meaning hazard ratio differs from SoA meaning heart rate). SoA rows with no descriptive text: Medical History (no protocol reference), Habits assessment, Height, Weight and Vital Signs (the referenced Section 12.2.3 lists patient demographics, preexisting conditions and historical illnesses but does not mention habits, height, weight or vital signs; no vital sign components, positions or units are stated anywhere except the SoA abbreviation line). Survival Information points to Section 10.1, which has no survival-specific text beyond the OS definition in 10.1.4. Skeletal-Related Events assessment points to Section 12.2.11; the SRE content is in subsection 12.2.11.6 and in 10.1.4 (TTFSRE). The Physical Examination entry records the SoA procedure category grouping only. Abbreviations under non-SoA tables/figures (Table JPBL.9.1, Figures 8.1/8.2, Table 10.1, Table 10.3) were not included. Updated 2026-11-07 to schema 1.1 (no re-extraction): instruments carry kind.
