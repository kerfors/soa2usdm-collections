NCT03421379 — protocol context

Protocol I8R-JE-IGBJ(a) (Amendment (a)), A Phase 3 Study of Nasal Glucagon (LY900018) Compared to Intramuscular Glucagon for Treatment of Insulin-induced Hypoglycemia in Japanese Patients with Diabetes Mellitus; Approval Date: 05-Dec-2017 · PDF pages 1-73 (PDF page numbers 1-73 of the protocol as posted on ClinicalTrials.gov (printed page numbers 1-72 start on PDF page 2))

Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readProtocol version (rule 11): the PDF holds a single protocol version, Amendment (a) of I8R-JE-IGBJ (approval 05-Dec-2017); the SoA (PDF pages 12-19) carries the I8R-JE-IGBJ(a) header, so everything is extracted from that version. Appendix 9 (pages 72-73) summarises what Amendment (a) changed against the original protocol: exclusion criterion [36] (retinopathy/maculopathy) added and the GlucaGen reconstitution volume changed from 1.0 mL to 1.1 mL; neither changes an SoA activity or a category here. Rule 10: the lab-tests table (Appendix 2, page 58) and the hepatic monitoring table (Appendix 4, page 62) run their two columns together in the text layer and were read from the page images before analytes were assigned. On page 58 the image confirms that Gamma-glutamyl transferase (GGT) and Insulin belong to Clinical Chemistry (the text layer interleaves them with Urinalysis), that HbA1c and Clinical Serology Test stand in the right column and Pregnancy test, FSH, Ethanol testing and Urine drug screen in the left. On page 62 the image shows Haptoglobin as its own item (the text layer puts it directly after the Hepatic Hematology heading) and Hepatic Chemistry as Total bilirubin, Conjugated bilirubin, Alkaline phosphatase, ALT, AST, GGT, CPK. Hematology lists Neutrophils to Basophils under 'Relative/% counts of:'. The hepatic monitoring panels are not SoA activities; they apply only in the event of a treatment-emergent hepatic abnormality (Section 9.5.5.2). No page lacks a text layer. Specimen is not stated for Hematology, Clinical Chemistry, Urinalysis, HbA1c, serology, FSH or ethanol testing. Pregnancy test: the SoA note says urine test 'at every admission period and follow-up visit', Appendix 2 footnote d says 'at every treatment period and follow-up visit' (same meaning, different wording). Clinical Serology Tests, FSH, Ethanol Testing and Urine Drug Screen: Appendix 2 footnote b says screening only (local laboratory). Abbreviations: Appendix 1 (pages 54-57) in full, plus ED and min (defined only under the SoA, page 19), RBC and WBC (Appendix 2 table, page 58) and AST, CPK, GGT, Ig, INR (Appendix 4 table, page 62). Every token in the SoA activity names (CRU, PG, ECG, HbA1c, FSH, PK, PD) is defined; LY900018 and IMG in SoA notes are the compound code and a defined abbreviation. Instruments: version, mode and respondent are not printed for any of the three; the Clarke survey and the Edinburgh scale are called self-report(ed) assessments. Units are not stated for any measurement group. SoA rows the text describes only through the SoA itself: Medical History (only 'screening medical history', page 26) and Ethanol Testing (only the Appendix 2 table). Clinical Serology Tests and HbA1c share the cross-reference note 'See Appendix 2, Clinical Laboratory Tests, for details.'; it is recorded once as in the input. Footnote a (immunogenicity follow-up every 12 weeks) is bound to no activity in the input; it repeats Section 9.7.1 (page 45). Updated 2026-11-07 to schema 1.1 (no re-extraction): instruments carry kind.
