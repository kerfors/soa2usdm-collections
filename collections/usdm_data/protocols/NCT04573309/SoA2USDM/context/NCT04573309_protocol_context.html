Extraction notes — conflicts, what was not found, pages that could not be readProtocol version (rule 11): the PDF holds a single version, Amendment 3.1 (US), 18 Mar 2022 (all pages); the SoA (pages 14-17) is from it. Pages 3 and 70-72 only summarize earlier amendments (e.g. Amendment 2 replaced the dosing diary with SMS text messaging for outpatient compliance); there is no later amendment in the PDF, so no additional entries. Page images checked (rule 10): page 60 (Table 9, two-column lab table) - the image confirms panel headings Clinical Chemistry, Hematology, Urinalysis, Other Tests and that the text-layer order (left cell then right cell per row) matches; no separate Coagulation heading: Prothrombin time, International normalized ratio and Partial thromboplastin time are printed under Hematology, while the SoA row reads 'Chemistry, hematology, Coagulation'. Potassium is printed twice in Clinical Chemistry (listed once here). Page 17 (Table 2) image confirms time points -0.5, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 12, 24 h marked for the combined PK/PD row. No page lacks a text layer. Lab: page 59 states all Table 9 tests are performed by the local laboratory; this includes the PK/PD copper/molybdenum analytes, which sections 8.1.1, 8.5 and 8.6 describe as measured by ICP-MS per the Laboratory Manual without naming a lab. Follicle-stimulating hormone and the alcohol (breath) test are SoA rows not listed in Table 9. Serum chemistry specimen from SoA note q. Conflicts: vital signs components - SoA note u and section 8.2.2 (page 45) include respiratory rate; page 46 lists temperature, systolic and diastolic blood pressure and heart rate only; safety endpoint (pages 10/23) says blood pressure and heart rate. ECG parameters - page 46 lists heart rate, PR, QRS, QT, QTc; page 53 lists heart rate, PR, RR, QRS, QT, QTcF; both recorded. Instruments: no questionnaire, rating scale or diary is named for an SoA activity. CTCAE v5.0 (AE severity grading by the Investigator, page 63) is recorded as the only instrument-like item, linked to 'Adverse events'; laboratory values are also graded by CTCAE (page 52). UWDRS is mentioned only in background (page 19). Eligibility scores (Leipzig criteria, MELD score, Modified Nazer score, pages 31-32) are inclusion/exclusion thresholds and are not recorded as instruments. Measurements: urination/bowel movement monitoring and intake monitoring (section 8.2.4.1-8.2.4.2) are collection records, not recorded as measurements; the menstruation check is described only by SoA note s ('for women only'). Abbreviations: Table 11 (pages 68-69) in full, plus those defined only under the SoA table (page 16: BMI, C-I, Cu, D, ECG, EOS/ET, HR, Mo, OP, PK = pharmacokinetics, UNS) and in the lab table (BUN, page 60). All tokens in SoA activity names (WD, BMI, PK, PD, Mo, PUF, Cu, LBC, HIV, ECG) are defined; ALXN1840 is the compound code (title page). SoA notes T1:m and T1:o are bound to section headers ('Administration of Study Intervention', 'PK/PD Analyses') that are not in soa_activities. SoA rows print 'Urine/serum pregnancy tests' and 'menstruation checks' (plural) vs the activity names. Updated 2026-11-07 to schema 1.1 (no re-extraction): instruments carry kind.
