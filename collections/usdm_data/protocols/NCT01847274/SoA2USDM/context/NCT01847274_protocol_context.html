EQ-5D-5L: the protocol prints 'scores ranging from 1 to 3' for a 5-level instrument; transcribed as printed. Rule 10: the lab tests (section 6.3.5, pp. 58-59) are a single-column bulleted list, not a multi-column table; p58 image checked, assignments to panels are as listed. Lab: p59 states 'These tests will be performed by the local laboratory ...' after the CBC/coagulation/chemistry/urinalysis lists; it is not stated whether serum CA-125 and serum pregnancy testing (named in the next sentence) are local, so lab is null for them. No central safety laboratory is named; gBRCA testing is central at Myriad and HRD tissue testing is 'centralized testing'. Conflicts: (1) Extended Visit Cycle vital signs/weight - Table 9 footnote 3 (p79) allows collection at local clinic or in-home nursing visit, while 7.4.2 (p80) says weight is 'only measured during in-clinic visits' and 7.4.1 lists vital signs and weight at the local clinic/in-home visit. (2) Coagulation is 'not required during the Extended Visit Schedule' (p58), and Table 9 has only 'Serum chemistry'. (3) Intensive PK subset size: approximately 12 (Table 7 footnote 20, p66), approximately 24 (synopsis p13), approximately 36 incl. 24 main + 12 FE (p61); triplicate-ECG subset approximately 24 (footnote 17) vs 36 (p60). (4) RECIST cadence after Cycle 14: every 6 cycles/24 weeks (pp. 6, 31, 66) vs every 3 cycles (7.2.7 p71, 7.4.2 p80). Instruments: RECIST v1.1 (Tumor Assessment) is response criteria, not recorded as an instrument; no instrument version is printed for FOSI, EQ-5D-5L or the neuropathy questionnaire. Measurements: no units or ECG components are printed; demographics and histories not recorded. SoA notes T3:4-T3:15 (Table 9 footnotes 4-15) are not printed in the source (Table 9 prints only footnotes 1-3); they are review annotations and were not used as evidence. Abbreviations: list pp. 22-24 (TEAE/TFST/TSST lines printed out of order on p24, paired as printed in order of terms); SoA-legend-only abbreviations C, CA, D, gBRCA, PET from Table 7 legend p64. Undefined tokens in SoA activity names: 'BRCA' (only within gBRCA = germline BRCA; BRCA itself is never expanded), '125' in CA-125 (CA = cancer antigen). SoA rows not described by a body section beyond being listed: 'Consumption of high fat meal, as applicable' is described in 3.1.2 (p34); 'Anti-cancer therapies assessment' and 'Anticancer therapies assessment' are two spellings of the same assessment (Table 7 vs Table 9), described in 7.2.9/7.4.4. p. 51 page image The EQ-5D-5L is a well validated general preference-based health-related quality of life (QoL) instrument in oncology as well as other conditions and is intended to compliment other QoL instruments. It was developed to assess health outcomes from a wide variety of interventions on a common scale for purposes of evaluation, allocation, and monitoring. The EQ-5D-5L encompasses 5 domains asking patients to rate their perceived health state today on the following dimensions: mobility, self-care, usual activities, pain/discomfort, and anxiety/depression. The EQ-5D-5L domains are scored on a Likert-type scale with scores ranging from 1 to 3
p. 51 page image In addition, a visual analog scale (VAS) is included in the EQ-5D-5L. The VAS measures current health status on a scale from 0 to 100
